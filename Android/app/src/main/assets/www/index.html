About

You stole MazerNazer's 10 corns and hid in a huge maze. Collect every corn, stay out of trouble, then find the exit on the east edge.

Controls

  • W A S Dmove (or crawl in vents)
  • Mouselook (drag if the cursor is not locked)
  • Shiftrun (uses stamina)
  • Venter / crawl / exit vents
  • 1 - 5pick a slot
  • E / Clickuse item
  • Mtoggle map
  • Kdive (when flooded)
  • Pmash to escape Grabber

Maze rules (read the posters)

  • No running
  • No drinking Coke
  • No grappling hook
  • No teleporting
  • No eating protein bars
  • No smoking

If the Police sees you break one, he doubles his speed and comes for you.

Items

  • Grapplepulls you far forward
  • Teleporterjump somewhere random
  • Cokeblasts people back
  • Protein Barrefills stamina
  • Glow Sticksee further, even in the dark
  • Smoke Bombeveryone nearby loses you
  • Banana Peeltrips whoever steps on it
  • Alarm Clocklures MazerNazer and HurterBoy
  • Spring Shoesrun faster for a while
  • Medkitstand up after a stab
  • Corn Radarmarks the nearest corn

Watch out

  • Policehandcuffs you for 30s
  • MazerNazerif he catches you, game over
  • HurterBoystabs you, then you wait for the Hospital Lady
  • Puppetdo not stare at it
  • Flood eventTunamies ram you down, Grabbers drag you under. Press K to dive and hide.

Events

Every minute or so MazerNazer drops a screen from the top corner and announces an event: box mania, blackouts, corn rain, zoomies, police raids, maze shuffles, naps, flicker nights, dizzy spells and more. Expect the unexpected.

Each colored room in the maze holds one corn.
