Turn your sound on. Click the game to lock the mouse.

About

You stole MazerNazer's 10 corns and hid in a huge maze. Collect every corn, stay out of trouble, then find the exit on the east edge.

Controls

  • W A S Dmove (or crawl in vents)
  • Mouselook (drag if the cursor is not locked)
  • Shiftrun (uses stamina)
  • Venter / crawl / exit vents
  • 1 - 5pick a slot
  • E / Clickuse item
  • Mtoggle map
  • Kdive (when flooded)
  • Pmash to escape Grabber

Maze rules (read the posters)

  • No running
  • No drinking Coke
  • No grappling hook
  • No teleporting
  • No eating protein bars
  • No smoking

If the Police sees you break one, he doubles his speed and comes for you.

Events

Every minute or so MazerNazer drops a screen from the top corner and announces an event: box mania, blackouts, corn rain, zoomies, police raids, maze shuffles, naps, flicker nights, dizzy spells and more. Expect the unexpected.

Each colored room in the maze holds one corn.
